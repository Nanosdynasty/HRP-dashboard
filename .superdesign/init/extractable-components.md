# Extractable components

## NewsFilterTabs

- Source: `static/index.html`
- Category: basic
- Description: Horizontal commercial-signal filter controls.
- Extractable props: `activeTopic`.

## NewsArticleCard

- Source: `static/js/app.js`
- Category: basic
- Description: Compact publisher, timestamp, relevance and article-title card.
- Extractable props: `title`, `source`, `provider`, `publishedAt`, `topics`, `relevanceReason`, `href`.
- Hardcoded: news card spacing, typography and accents.
